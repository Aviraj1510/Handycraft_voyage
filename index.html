No. 02 — Origins

Crafting memories that last forever.

HandyCraft Voyage is a small handmade studio based in Ahmedabad. Every hoop is stitched by hand, one thread at a time — no machines, no shortcuts, no two pieces exactly alike.

We work almost entirely on custom orders: your names, your wedding date, your favourite colours, turned into something you can hang on a wall or gift to someone you love. You're not just buying décor — you're taking home a one-of-a-kind memory.
